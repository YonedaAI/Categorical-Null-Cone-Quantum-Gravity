Categorical Null-Cone
Quantum Gravity

Can two constructions of a gravitational observable agree when an observer’s causal diamond grows?

Research status

In a model with positive area, changing the order of two operations changes their product by a phase. One operation probes the logarithm of the area at a marked cut. The other rescales the area. The phase survives when the model keeps the cut marked and adds an independent system.

The correspondence for a gravitational diamond remains open. In Observables as Cohomology, the classical observables commute under multiplication. A product-preserving map from that algebra cannot reach every observable in the noncommutative null-cone algebra. The next step is to construct quantum probes from gravitational data and compare their products as the diamond grows.
