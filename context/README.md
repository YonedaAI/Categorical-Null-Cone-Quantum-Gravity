## Further primary literature

- Brunetti, Fredenhagen and Verch define covariant algebras for admissible spacetime embeddings. This provides a variance convention and makes clear what an inclusion must do to an observable. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0112041)
- Donnelly and Freidel construct extended phase spaces with surface degrees of freedom. The matching of boundary charges matters when two subsystems are joined. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.04744)
- Lehner, Myers, Poisson and Sorkin analyze parametrization dependence of null boundary contributions and the choice of joint terms compatible with additivity. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.00207)
- R. D. Jordan derives causal equations for expectation values from a time contour with two branches. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.33.444)
